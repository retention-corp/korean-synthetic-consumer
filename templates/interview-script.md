# Interview Script Template

Use this to convert synthetic findings into real user interview questions.

## Follow up on the participant's last answer

When the participant corrects an assumption, use the issue they named.
Anchor the next question in one specific past occasion and ask what they
actually did then. If they have already described an action, ask about the
next step in that same occasion. Leave room for them to say they did nothing.

Ask one question. A Korean pattern is “가장 최근에 [참여자가 말한 상황]이 있었을 때, 실제로 어떻게 하셨어요?”
Use the participant's words for the situation. Save questions about reasons,
general habits, or hypothetical solutions for later.

## Warm-up
- Tell me about the last time you handled this problem.
- What did you do instead?

## Problem Discovery
- What made it painful or expensive?
- Who else was involved?
- What happens if you do nothing?

## Concept Probe
- What part sounds useful?
- What part sounds unrealistic?
- What would you need to see before trying it?

## Buying/Adoption
- Who approves this?
- What budget would it come from?
- What would make you stop using it?

Avoid leading questions. Use synthetic personas to prepare, not to replace real interviews.
